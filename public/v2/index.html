Findings

what I have been thinking about · what came out of it

One question, worked on in the open: how should AI agents share what they know, and who gets to act on it? The paper is the current answer. The figures are the evidence it rests on. The ledger is how it got here.

v2.2current revisionSeptember 2026position paper

The Synthetic Membrane: a coordination layer for multi-agent AI systems

A coordination contract for shared evidence, selective access and action ownership between agents, with explicit limits and an evaluation plan. The claim is bounded: if a controlled blackboard matches the membrane with lower overhead, the separate abstraction has not earned its place.

  • hypothesisBounded, with a prior-art comparison against protocols, frameworks and the blackboard.
  • contractAuthority and consistency rules for who may read, contest and act on shared state.
  • worked exampleA suspected ransomware incident, run through the contract with its limits stated.
  • evaluationMatched baselines, cost measured at the model boundary, and mechanism ablations.
